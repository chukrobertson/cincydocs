ABOUT CINCYDOCS

A straightforward local notary service.

Cincinnati Document Services is operated by an Ohio-commissioned notary and provides in-person mobile notary appointments in Cincinnati and surrounding Ohio communities.

Clear arrangements before the appointment

Each request starts with the basics: your general location, document type, number of signers, and preferred timing. Before an appointment is accepted, we confirm availability, the meeting location, identification requirements, and total fees.

Service at an agreed Ohio location

Mobile appointments are arranged in Cincinnati and surrounding Ohio communities, subject to availability and travel arrangements. Customers should wait for confirmation before traveling to a meeting location.

Notarization without document preparation

You bring the document and the required identification. The document issuer or a qualified legal professional can answer questions about the document’s legal effect and identify the notarial act you need.

Cincinnati Document Services does not prepare legal documents, select a notarial act for a customer, or provide legal advice.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Share only what is needed to arrange the meeting.

Start with general appointment details. Do not email identification images, Social Security numbers, financial information, medical records, or sensitive document contents.

Before the appointment: Confirm the document type, identification requirements, location, timing, and fees.

At the appointment: Each signer appears in person with the document and required identification.

After the appointment: Keep your completed document and any instructions provided by its issuer.
