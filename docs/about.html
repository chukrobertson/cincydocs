ABOUT CINCYDOCS

Useful work. Carefully done.

CincyDocs turns messy documents, spreadsheets, and information into clean, organized, ready-to-use results.

Help with the work that keeps getting pushed aside

A folder of scans. A spreadsheet that has grown difficult to use. A research task that needs a clear source list. CincyDocs offers practical project-based help for people and small businesses who need the work completed without hiring an employee.

Based in Cincinnati, CincyDocs serves individuals, independent contractors, small businesses, and organizations remotely throughout the United States. Local mobile notary appointments remain available in the Cincinnati area.

A straightforward way to work together

Each project starts with a conversation about the outcome you need. We agree on the deliverables, price, and deadline before work begins, using primarily asynchronous communication so you can provide details and review progress when it fits your schedule.

Care, confidentiality, and accuracy

Work is performed from copies, originals are preserved, and unclear information is flagged. File handling, confidentiality, and retention requirements are discussed before records are shared. You receive the agreed files and the explanations included in your project scope.

Cincinnati Document Services does not prepare legal documents or provide legal advice. Research services organize publicly available information and do not include legal research services.
