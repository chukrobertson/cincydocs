PRICING

Know the arrangement before you meet

Notarial acts

Up to $5 per act

Ohio permits up to $5 for a traditional notarial act. The fee is not calculated per signature.

Mobile travel

Quoted first

A reasonable travel fee may be charged separately and must be agreed upon before the notarial act.

Fee reference: Ohio Revised Code § 147.08.
