HOW IT WORKS

Three steps to finished work

Projects are handled primarily through asynchronous communication. Share details and review updates when it fits your schedule.

  1. 01

    Describe Your Project

    Tell us what needs fixing, converting, organizing, or researching. Describe your files first; share redacted samples when appropriate after we agree on a transfer method.

  2. 02

    Receive a Quote

    We’ll review the scope and provide a price, expected deliverables, and proposed completion date. Work starts after those details are agreed.

  3. 03

    Get Your Finished Work

    Receive organized, ready-to-use files and an explanation of the work completed when appropriate. Any unresolved questions are flagged for your review.

CAREFUL DOCUMENT HANDLING

Start with a description.

Please do not send confidential records, Social Security numbers, medical records, account details, or unredacted identification through ordinary email or this intake form.

Before files are shared: We agree on an appropriate transfer method and any confidentiality, handling, or retention requirements.

During the work: Work is performed from copies. Source files remain unchanged, and unclear values are flagged for review.

At handoff: Receive the files and supporting notes defined in the agreed scope.
