PRIMARY SERVICE

❧ Data Cleanup & Preparation❧

Spreadsheet and dataset cleanup, normalization, consolidation, validation, and documentation.

What cleanup can include

  • Remove and investigate duplicates
  • Standardize column names
  • Normalize categories
  • Standardize dates and formats
  • Identify missing or malformed values
  • Combine multiple files or worksheets
  • Split or merge fields
  • Restructure poorly organized tables
  • Clean CSV and Excel exports
  • Preserve identifiers correctly
  • Identify suspicious or inconsistent records
  • Prepare datasets for analysis, import, reporting, or archival use
  • Document significant transformations

Ambiguous values are not invented. When a value cannot be safely determined, it is preserved or flagged for review rather than silently "fixed."

DOCUMENT HANDLING

Clear expectations from first message to handoff.

Work is scoped and priced before it begins. The appropriate transfer method and relevant handling or retention details are agreed before sensitive files are shared.

Before sharing files

  • Use ordinary email or text to describe the project, not to send Social Security numbers, medical records, financial account details, unredacted identification, or similarly sensitive material.
  • Agree on an appropriate transfer method before sending sensitive information.
  • Let us know if the records contain sensitive information so handling and retention can be scoped.

Protect the originals

  • Retain your original files and backup copies.
  • Work is performed from working copies; source files are not modified destructively.
  • Ambiguous values are preserved or flagged for review, not invented.

Cincinnati Document Services is not a law firm, accounting firm, government agency, or financial adviser. We do not determine which legal form you should use.
