Application for Disability Insurance Benefits

Form SSA-16-BK
OFFICIAL FORM Social Security Administration United States ⚠ ELEVATED REVIEW

❧ About This Form

If you cannot work because of a medical condition expected to last at least one year or result in death, you may use this form to apply for SSDI benefits. Most people apply online or by phone, but the paper form is available if needed.

Application for Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI) benefits. Available in English and Spanish.
