PRICING

Fees are confirmed before the appointment

Notarial acts

Up to $5 per act

For an in-person notarization, Ohio permits a fee of up to $5 for each notarial act. The fee is not calculated per signature.

Mobile travel

Quoted first

A reasonable travel fee may be charged separately. It will be disclosed and agreed upon before the notarial act.

Official fee reference: Ohio Revised Code § 147.08.
