BEFORE YOUR APPOINTMENT

Confirm the details first

Location and availability

Share your area, preferred date and time, document type, and number of signers. The meeting location and appointment time must be confirmed in advance.

Identification and signing

Signers must appear in person for the mobile appointment. Identification must meet Ohio requirements. Confirm acceptable identification, witness needs, and signing instructions before the appointment.

Fees and travel

For in-person notarization, Ohio authorizes a fee of up to $5 per notarial act, not per signature. Any reasonable travel fee is separate, disclosed, and agreed before the notarial act.

Ohio Revised Code § 147.08
