# Revenue Phase 2

Phase 2 is intentionally documented, not implemented in the launch revision. Priorities are ordered by the expected ability to help win the first paying projects without requiring new capital.

1. **Dedicated service detail pages**
   Add focused pages for SOPs and business documentation, writing and copy, fillable forms and workflows, spreadsheets/research/dashboards, and document/media work. Each page should end with a project-description CTA.
2. **Real portfolio samples**
   Add before/after examples only from work the owner can verify and is permitted to publish. Label each sample accurately and remove identifying information as needed.
3. **Starter packages or pricing guidance**
   Publish pricing only after actual time, software, handoff, and revision costs are confirmed. Avoid estimates that are not grounded in the business's real capacity.
4. **A secure intake and file-transfer workflow**
   Choose and document an appropriate transfer method before asking customers to send sensitive files. Do not imply security features until the workflow is actually selected and tested.
5. **Invoicing and payment options**
   Add an invoicing and payment process after the owner confirms the preferred provider, fees, records, and refund or revision terms.
6. **Permission-based testimonials**
   Add real testimonials only after the customer gives permission for the exact wording and attribution.
7. **Google Business Profile alignment**
   Align the profile, service descriptions, contact details, and local handoff language with the launch site.
8. **Local SEO and sitemap improvements**
   Add service-area detail, structured internal linking, and a sitemap after the service pages and local operating details are stable.
9. **Privacy-conscious analytics**
   Decide whether analytics are necessary. If used, document the privacy choice, data retention, and consent approach before installing anything.
10. **A clearer notary appointment workflow**
    Define the appointment intake, location and travel-feasibility check, identification requirements, fees, and confirmation steps before expanding notary visibility.
